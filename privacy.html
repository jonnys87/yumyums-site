Privacy policy

YumYums collects no personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no server belonging to us for anything to be sent to.

Last updated: 30 September 2026. Applies to YumYums for iOS and iPadOS, version 1.1.0.

What YumYums stores, and where

Your recipes, their photos, your meal plan and your shopping list are written to storage belonging to the app, on your iPhone or iPad. They are sent to your own private iCloud database only if you enable sync. They are included in your device backup, whether that is iCloud Backup or an encrypted backup to your own computer, because that is how iOS backs up every app. That backup is between you and Apple; we have no access to it.

Deleting the app removes its local data; it does not by itself erase an existing iCloud copy or your device backups. "Erase all recipes and lists" in Settings inside the app removes the app’s recipes and lists, and if you have sync switched on that erases the synced copy in your iCloud and on your other devices. Copies you exported and existing device backups are managed separately.

iCloud sync, if you switch it on

YumYums can keep your library in step across your own iPhone and iPad. It is off when you install the app and there is a switch for it in Settings. While it is off, the app does not sync your library to iCloud. iCloud Backup is a separate iOS setting.

With it on, your recipes and their photographs, your meal plan and your shopping list are written to your own private iCloud database, in your own Apple Account, using your own iCloud storage. That is a place only you can read. We have no account there, no key, no dashboard and no way to see any of it, and there is still nothing to sign up for with us. Apple's own privacy policy governs the storage, in the same way it governs your photos and your device backup.

Turning the switch off stops the syncing straight away and leaves everything already on that device exactly where it is. It does not delete what is in iCloud, because your other device may still be using it; deleting that is what "Erase all recipes and lists" does, or you can remove YumYums from iCloud in the Settings app under your name.

When YumYums uses the network

These network uses follow from features you choose:

  • Fetching a recipe page. When you paste a link, share one in, or open one from a batch, the app requests that page over HTTPS so it can read the recipe out of it.
  • Fetching that recipe's photo. Downloaded once and kept on your device so the recipe still has a picture when the site changes.
  • Syncing with your own iCloud, and only if you have turned that switch on. That traffic goes to Apple, on your behalf, into storage only you can read. See the section above.
  • Preparing hands-free speech recognition. Apple’s speech framework may download a language model when you first enable hands-free. Once ready, recognition runs on your device; your microphone audio is not uploaded.

Page and photo requests go to the recipe site, not to us. The site sees them the way it sees any web browser: an IP address, a request for a page, the time of day. That is a normal consequence of visiting a web page and it is governed by that site's own privacy policy, not this one. Nothing about you or your library is attached to the request.

Your saved library, the meal plan, the shopping list, search and Cook Mode work with the device in flight mode, sync switched on or not: syncing is something the app catches up on when there is a connection. Hands-free needs its language model installed first, and link import needs a connection to fetch the page.

The on-device AI import

Reading a screenshot or a piece of pasted text uses Apple's Foundation Models framework, which runs the model on the device. The text of the page, the words read off your screenshots, and everything the model produces stay on the device. No prompt and no result is sent to Apple, to us, or to any other service. There is no API key in this app because there is no API being called.

Permissions

Photos
YumYums never asks for access to your photo library. The screenshot import uses the system picker, which runs outside the app and hands over only the specific images you selected, whether for screenshot import or adding your own recipe photos. The app cannot see anything else in your library and does not hold a permission to try.
Reminders
Only if you choose "Export to Reminders" on the shopping list. iOS asks you first. If you allow it, YumYums writes your unchecked shopping items into a Reminders list called "Shopping list" on your device. It does not read your existing reminder items. Your chosen Reminders account may sync that list through its own service. If you decline, the shopping list carries on working inside the app.
Microphone and speech recognition
Requested when you enable hands-free in Cook Mode. Audio is processed on your device to recognise cooking commands. YumYums does not save recordings or send the audio to a server. Hands-free starts off each time you open Cook Mode; you can stop it at any time. If you decline permission, you can still use the on-screen cooking controls.
Notifications and Live Activities
The first timer asks for notification permission so YumYums can alert you when it finishes. These are local alerts, not server messages. If you decline, the timer still counts down without a notification alert. A Live Activity can show running timers on the Lock Screen, subject to your device settings.
Anything else
No location, contacts, camera, health data or calendar access.

What is not in this app

  • No accounts, sign-ins, or email addresses collected.
  • No analytics or usage tracking of any kind.
  • No crash reporting service.
  • No advertising, no advertising identifier, no App Tracking Transparency prompt, because there is nothing to track.
  • No third-party software development kits at all. The app is written against Apple's frameworks and nothing else.
  • No data sold, shared, or transferred to anybody, because none is collected in the first place.

Children

YumYums is rated 4+ and collects nothing from anyone, of any age. It has no chat, no user-to-user features and no in-app purchases. Opening an original recipe hands its address to Safari, where your own Screen Time settings apply.

Your rights

Data protection law gives you rights to access, correct, export and erase personal data that a company holds about you. We hold none, so there is nothing to request and no request to make. Your recipes are already in your possession, on your device.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of YumYums does something that changes any of the above, this page will be updated before that version ships and the date at the top will change. The iCloud section above is an example: sync was added after the first draft of this page, and the page changed with it rather than after somebody asked. A version already on your phone cannot start collecting data: that would take an update, which you choose to install.
